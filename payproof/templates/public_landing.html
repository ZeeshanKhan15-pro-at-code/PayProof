PayProof

A plausible invoice can request a different payment destination. Compare the new instructions with information you previously trusted, inspect the evidence and independently check any change.

VERIFY A PAYMENT REQUEST

No login. Start with your own previously trusted information and current request in an isolated temporary workspace.

Public hackathon workspace: use synthetic or test data. This is not confidential enterprise storage. Anonymous workspaces expire after 30 minutes of inactivity; restarting the service ends access. Source review, comparison and human attestation are separate. PayProof does not authorize payment or establish account ownership.

Two inputs, two roles

  1. Choose or deliberately establish previously trusted payment information and a known-good contact.
  2. Paste the current email/invoice/notice or upload supported UTF8 .txt / raw .eml text.
  3. Review extracted evidence, run comparison and record a separate independent human check where applicable.

Supported now: pasted text and UTF8 .txt/.eml files. EML is read as raw text; MIME, attachments, PDF, MSG and OCR are not parsed. No file type support is implied by the upload control.
